THE BRAND

Designed to make you feel special.

Beads By Rumi is a handmade accessories brand focused on beaded bags, bracelets and statement pieces. We love the way a collection of tiny beads can become something elegant, expressive and completely personal.

Each piece is assembled with attention to bead placement, texture, color and finish. From classic pearls to sparkling crystals, our designs are made for gifting, celebrations and everyday moments when you want a little more beauty.
