OUR APPROACH

Craftsmanship first.

Beads by Rumi began with a love of creating beaded pieces for family. What started as a personal creative passion grew into a handmade brand focused on thoughtful design and carefully finished beadwork.

We believe handmade luxury is in the details: the consistency of the beadwork, the strength of the thread, the finishing, and the care given to every order.

Because many pieces are made to order, customers can also discuss colors, accents and custom requirements with us.
